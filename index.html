Gameplay Loop

  1. 1) Buat avatar & pilih mode Single/Multiplayer.
  2. 2) Jalan di Map DIY (top-down) ke lokasi heritage.
  3. 3) Baca story mode + info HTM + jam operasional.
  4. 4) Selesaikan Quick Quiz, Time Challenge, Puzzle.
  5. 5) Kumpulkan XP, badge, buka Road Trip Challenge.
